Privacy Policy

Life Assist  |  Effective Date: November 18, 2026  |  Version 1.0

1. Introduction

Life Assist is a personal life-management application designed to help you organize and track personal records, reminders, bills, documents, appointments, warranties, subscriptions, vehicles, and tasks.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, stored, used, and managed when you use the Life Assist application.

2. Information We Store

Depending on the features you use, information you enter may include:

  • Personal information and account details.
  • Bills and payment-related records.
  • Documents, attachments, and expiry dates.
  • Vehicle and maintenance details.
  • Subscription information.
  • Warranty information.
  • Appointments and related details.
  • Tasks and reminders.
  • Personal notes and records.
  • Account and authentication information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to:

  • Organize and manage your personal records.
  • Display upcoming activities and important information.
  • Provide reminders and notifications.
  • Track expiry dates, deadlines, and payment schedules.
  • Manage bills, documents, vehicles, subscriptions, warranties, and appointments.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve application functionality and user experience.

4. Data Storage

Depending on the feature, information may be stored locally on your device and/or processed or stored by backend services.

Life Assist uses local storage and backend functionality to support its features. Storage arrangements may differ depending on the record type and functionality being used.

5. Notifications

If enabled, Life Assist may send notifications to remind you about scheduled activities, including:

  • Upcoming deadlines.
  • Document expiry dates.
  • Bill payment dates.
  • Appointments.
  • Vehicle maintenance schedules.
  • Subscription renewals.
  • Warranty expiry dates.
  • Other reminders created within the application.

Notification delivery depends on your device settings, permissions, and system availability. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

6. Third-Party Services

Life Assist uses third-party services to support application functionality.

  • Google Sign-In: Used for user authentication and account access.
  • Firebase: Used for application initialization and related services.
  • Life Assist Backend Services: Used to process and manage information required for application features.

These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective functionality and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

7. Data Sharing

  • We do not intentionally sell or rent your personal information.
  • Information may be processed by authentication providers, backend services, and other services used by Life Assist when necessary to provide application functionality.
  • We do not intentionally share personal information with third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

8. User Responsibilities

You are responsible for:

  • Providing accurate information when using the application.
  • Keeping your account credentials secure.
  • Managing your personal records and reminders.
  • Maintaining independent copies of important documents and records where appropriate.

Life Assist is intended to help organize personal information and should not be considered the sole backup location for important documents.

9. Data Deletion

You can remove individual records using the deletion controls available within the application.

Currently, Life Assist does not provide an in-app account deletion option.

Removing a record from the application may not automatically remove copies already stored by backend services or third-party providers.

For account-related privacy requests or assistance with data deletion, please contact us using the contact information provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

Life Assist is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate authorization. If we become aware of such collection, we will take reasonable steps to address it.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in application functionality, services, or applicable requirements.

Updated information will be published on this webpage, together with a revised effective date where applicable.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
